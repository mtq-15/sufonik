FAQ

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Start free, connect your store, and only enter payment when you go live on a paid plan.

What counts as an "AI conversation"?

A visitor session that reaches at least one AI answer. Escalated sessions and unanswered messages are not counted against your quota.

Which stores does it work with?

Any store that can load JavaScript — Shopify, WooCommerce, or fully custom/headless stacks. If your store has an API, we can add live order lookup.

Is my data used for training?

Never. Your catalog, FAQ, and conversations stay yours. Data is TLS-encrypted and access is scoped per store.

What if a question is out of the assistant's depth?

It escalates to a human with full conversation context. Nothing falls through the cracks.
